**Version**: 1.0  
**Effective Date**: [DATE]  
**Last Updated**: 2026-09-30  

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between:

**Data Controller**: [Customer Name], located at [Address] ("Customer")  
**Data Processor**: Durkan AS / Nordstrand Technologies, located at [Address] ("Processor")

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 This DPA supplements the Master Service Agreement ("MSA") between Customer and Processor regarding Paperclip services ("Services").

1.2 The parties acknowledge that:
- Customer is the data controller
- Processor is the data processor
- Processor processes personal data on behalf of Customer as necessary to provide the Services
- This DPA reflects the parties' commitment to comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable data protection laws

1.3 **Scope of Processing**: This DPA applies to any personal data processed by Processor when providing Services to Customer, including:
- User account information
- Company configurations
- Agent deployment data
- Execution logs and activity
- Any personal data included in customer data uploaded to Paperclip

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

2.1 **Customer Responsibilities**:
- Determine what personal data is processed and for what purpose
- Ensure lawful basis exists for processing
- Provide necessary privacy notices to data subjects
- Comply with data subject rights requests
- Ensure compliance with applicable data protection laws

2.2 **Processor Responsibilities**:
- Process data only as instructed by Customer
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures
- Maintain records of processing activities
- Assist Customer in meeting data subject rights
- Notify Customer of data breaches
- Maintain this DPA in current form

- Usage data (login times, actions)

3.2 **Categories of Data Subjects**:
[To be specified by Customer, for example:]
- Company employees and users
- End users of customer services

3.3 **Processing Activities**:
The Processor processes personal data for the following purposes:
- Providing Paperclip platform services

- Security and fraud prevention
- Compliance with legal obligations
- Service improvement and analytics (anonymized)

3.4 **Duration of Processing**:
Personal data is processed for the duration of the MSA and for [retention period] afterward unless Customer requests earlier deletion.

## 4. Instructions and Authority

4.1 **Scope of Instructions**: Customer instructs Processor to process personal data only:
- As necessary to provide the Services
- As documented in this DPA and the MSA
- In accordance with applicable law
- To the extent needed to fulfill Customer's legitimate purposes

4.2 **Authorization**: Processor may process personal data only upon receipt of documented instructions from Customer. Additional processing requires a written amendment to this DPA.

4.3 **Objections**: If Processor believes a Customer instruction violates applicable law, Processor must notify Customer immediately and may suspend processing pending clarification.

## 5. Technical and Organizational Measures

5.1 Processor implements appropriate security measures including:

**Technical Measures**:
- TLS encryption for data in transit
- Encryption at rest for sensitive data
- Access controls and authentication (including 2FA for board users)
- Regular security patching and updates
- Network segmentation and firewalls
- Intrusion detection and prevention systems

**Organizational Measures**:
- Data protection training for personnel
- Confidentiality agreements with employees
- Incident response procedures
- Vendor management and due diligence
- Regular security audits
- Documented information security policies

5.2 **Risk Assessment**: The level of security is appropriate to the risk of processing personal data within Paperclip.

5.3 **Updates**: Processor will update security measures as technology and threats evolve.

## 6. Sub-processors

6.1 Customer authorizes Processor to engage sub-processors listed in `doc/subprocessor-list.md`.

6.2 **Sub-processor Changes**:
- Processor will notify Customer at least 30 days before adding or removing sub-processors
- Customer may object to new sub-processors on legitimate grounds
- If Customer objects and parties cannot resolve within 10 days, Customer may terminate affected Services without penalty

6.3 **Sub-processor Obligations**: Processor ensures all sub-processors:
- Sign equivalent data processing agreements
- Implement appropriate security measures
- Comply with data protection obligations
- Agree to audit and assessment rights

## 7. Data Subject Rights

7.1 **Assistance with Subject Requests**: Processor will assist Customer in fulfilling data subject rights requests, including:
- Right of access (copies of personal data)
- Right to correction (rectification)
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object

7.2 **Procedures**:
- Customer will submit requests to [support channel]
- Processor will process reasonable requests at no additional cost
- Processor will comply within [timeframe, e.g., 10 business days] where feasible
- Processor may decline unreasonable requests

7.3 **Data Subject Contacts**: Data subjects should contact Customer (the controller) first. Processor will assist Customer in responding.

## 8. Security Incident and Breach Notification

8.1 **Notification Obligation**: Processor will notify Customer immediately (and in no case later than 24 hours) upon discovery of:
- Unauthorized access to personal data
- Accidental or unlawful destruction of personal data
- Loss of personal data
- Any suspected security incident

8.2 **Breach Notice Content**: Notification will include:
- Nature of the breach
- Affected data categories and individuals
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken or proposed to address the breach
- Contact for further information

8.3 **Investigation Cooperation**: Processor will cooperate with Customer and regulators in investigating breaches.

8.4 **Liability**: Processor remains liable for breaches caused by Processor's failure to comply with this DPA or applicable law.

## 9. Audit Rights and Compliance

9.1 **Audit Rights**: Customer (or Customer's auditor) may audit Processor's compliance with this DPA:
- Upon reasonable notice (at least 15 days)
- No more than once per calendar year
- At Customer's expense unless audit reveals non-compliance

9.2 **Certifications**: Processor will provide copies of:
- SOC 2 reports or equivalent
- ISO 27001 certification (if applicable)
- Penetration test reports
- Other relevant security certifications

9.3 **Records**: Processor maintains records of all processing activities and will provide summaries to Customer upon request.

## 10. Return and Deletion of Data

10.1 Upon termination or expiration of the MSA:
- Customer may request return of all personal data in commonly used format
- Processor will delete all personal data within 30 days unless law requires retention
- Deletion will be certified in writing to Customer
- Backups will be deleted per normal retention schedule

10.2 **Exceptions**: Processor may retain personal data:
- If required by applicable law
- For backup purposes (with equivalent security)
- For audit trail purposes (anonymized where possible)

## 11. Transfer of Data Outside the EEA

11.1 **Data Transfers**: If Processor transfers personal data outside the EEA:
- Processor ensures appropriate safeguards per GDPR Chapter 5
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) are in place
- Transfer Impact Assessments are conducted
- Supplementary measures are implemented if necessary

11.2 **Notification**: Processor will notify Customer of any changes to transfer mechanisms.

## 12. Liability and Remedies

12.1 **Processor Liability**: Processor is liable for damages caused by breach of this DPA or violation of GDPR.

12.2 **Exceptions to Liability**: Processor is not liable for:
- Breaches caused by Customer's instructions
- Breaches caused by Customer's failure to follow Processor's security recommendations
- Foreseeable damages were excluded in the MSA

12.3 **Damage Caps**: Liability limitations in the MSA apply to data protection claims, except where prohibited by law.

## 13. Data Protection Impact Assessment

13.1 Processor will assist Customer in:
- Conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA) if required
- Consulting with relevant data protection authorities
- Documenting processing activities

13.2 Processor will provide relevant information about processing upon request.

## 14. International Data Transfers and Compliance

14.1 **GDPR Compliance**: Both parties comply with GDPR including:
- Legal basis for processing
- Data subject rights
- Lawful transfer mechanisms
- Data Protection Authority cooperation

14.2 **CCPA Compliance**: Processor assists Customer in complying with CCPA including:
- Consumer privacy rights
- Opt-out mechanisms
- Data sale restrictions
- Service provider obligations

14.3 **Other Regulations**: Processor will comply with applicable data protection laws in jurisdictions where Customer operates.

## 15. Term and Termination

15.1 **Term**: This DPA continues for the duration of the MSA.

15.2 **Termination**: Either party may terminate this DPA:
- Upon 30 days' written notice
- Immediately if the other party materially breaches and does not cure within 15 days
- Upon termination of the MSA

15.3 **Post-Termination**: Section 10 (Return and Deletion) applies.
